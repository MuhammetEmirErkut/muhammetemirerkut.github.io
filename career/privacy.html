Gizlilik Politikası

Our commitment to protecting your personal information and application data.

Last Updated: January 2026

Information We Collect

CareerLift collects minimal personal information necessary to provide our service. Most of your content is stored securely on your own device:

  • CV data you create (stored locally on your device)
  • App usage analytics (fully anonymous)
  • Subscription status (managed securely by Apple App Store)

1. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data to run and enhance the user experience. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide CV building, rating, and editing services.
  • Deliver premium templates and AI-powered features for subscribers.
  • Improve app performance, stability, and troubleshoot bugs.
  • Process subscription payments (handled automatically by Apple's payment system).

2. Data Storage & Ownership

Your CV data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your personal CV details, descriptions, or contact information on our servers unless you explicitly opt in to use cloud sync features.

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with a limited number of trusted third-party providers for billing and crash reporting:

  • Apple App Store: Processes all transaction billing, license validation, and subscriptions.
  • Apple Analytics: Provides anonymous usage graphs to identify screen loading failures (opt-out available in iOS system settings).

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your information. All networks and data transmission routes are encrypted using SSL/TLS industry protocols.

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have absolute rights over your data. You may:

  • Access and review your stored CV parameters inside the app.
  • Modify or permanently delete any CV template data at any time.
  • Export your CV data in PDF format.
  • Manage, pause, or terminate premium subscriptions within Apple ID settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect or store any information from individuals under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect operational changes. Changes will take effect immediately upon being posted on this website page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, please contact us by emailing developer support at erkutmuhammetemir@gmail.com.
