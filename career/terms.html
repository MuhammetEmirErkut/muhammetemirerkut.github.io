CareerLift Premium Subscription

By subscribing to our Premium tier, you unlock advanced capabilities:

  • Service Title: CareerLift Premium Features
  • Subscription Length: Available on Monthly or Yearly billing cycles
  • Pricing: $4.99 per Month or $59.99 per Year (subject to local currencies and taxes)
  • Content & Services: Unlimited resume templates, interactive interview prep guides, advanced AI suggestions, and priority developer support

1. Subscription & Billing

Your subscription payment will be charged directly to your Apple ID Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions are billed automatically based on your chosen term (monthly or yearly) unless auto-renew is explicitly disabled.

2. Auto-Renewal Policy

Subscriptions auto-renew automatically. To prevent renewal charges, you must disable the auto-renewal feature at least 24 hours prior to the conclusion of your current subscription period. Your account will be processed for renewal payment within 24 hours before the end of the active term.

3. Cancellation Procedure

You can review, manage, or terminate your active subscription plans anytime by accessing your Apple ID Account Settings on your iOS device. Note that cancellations will take effect at the conclusion of the active billing cycle; no partial refunds are provided for early cancellation.

4. Payments & Refunds

All subscription transactions and refund requests are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. under the standard App Store guidelines and terms. To request refunds, please refer to the Apple Support system.

5. Service Adjustments

CareerLift reserves the right to modify template varieties, pricing tiers, feature lists, or discontinue certain options with reasonable notice to active subscribers.

6. Contact Details

For questions or issues regarding our subscription services, terms, or policies, please reach out to developer support at muhammetemir.erkut@gmail.com.
