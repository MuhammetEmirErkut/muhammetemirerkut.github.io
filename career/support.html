❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding CareerLift and its features:

How is my resume and career data saved?

All resume details, drafts, and application tracking entries are stored directly and securely in your device's local CoreData storage. You can export your documents as a formatted PDF at any time.

How do I delete my data or reset the app?

CareerLift follows a privacy-first, local-only architecture. Removing the application from your device permanently deletes all locally stored data and resume files.

What features are included in CareerLift Pro?

Subscribing to Pro unlocks unlimited AI-assisted resume tailoring, automated cover letter generation, ATS keyword optimization scans, unlimited resume template styles, and high-resolution PDF exports.

How does the AI optimization engine work?

The AI assistant analyzes your inputted work experience and target job descriptions to enhance professional wording, emphasize impactful bullet points, and ensure alignment with industry-standard hiring guidelines.
