Overview

Muhammet Emir Erkut ("we", "our", or "us") is deeply committed to protecting your privacy while using the Komisyon 101 mobile application ("App"). All financial calculations are processed locally on your device and are never stored on external cloud servers.

1. Information We Collect

The App processes minimal data necessary to provide its core calculation features and ensure stability:

  • User Inputted Data: Any sale amount, POS commission rate, installment count, or note inputted into the calculator is processed strictly on your local device. This data is never transmitted to remote servers.
  • Anonymous Technical Telemetry: To identify application crashes and maintain performance, non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as OS version, device model, and crash logs via standard SDKs like Google Firebase) may be collected.

2. How We Use Information

Any technical data processed is utilized strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining core mathematical calculations accurately.
  • Diagnosing crashes, software bugs, and performance bottlenecks.
  • Improving usability, interface speed, and features in future updates.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial calculation data with third parties. Anonymous telemetry may only be shared with industry-standard analytics/crash reporting platforms to sustain application reliability.

4. Data Security

We employ modern security practices to protect your data locally on your device. However, please note that no electronic transmission or local storage system is 100% immune to vulnerabilities.

5. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
